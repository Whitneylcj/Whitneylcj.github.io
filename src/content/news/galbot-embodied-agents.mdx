In September 2026, I joined **Galbot** as a research intern working on the embodied intelligence brain.

My work explores agentic approaches to helping robots reason about tasks and carry them out: connecting language-model decisions with actions and feedback from the world.

This is part of my broader interest in agentic reinforcement learning, LLM decision making, and learning from interaction.
